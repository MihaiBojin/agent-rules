## Judge the request

A request states the goal. How to reach it is your call. When the docs or research show that it cannot work
as asked, or that it asks for something deprecated, wrong or non-idiomatic, build the best approach that meets the goal
and do not stop to ask. In the reply, say what you did instead and why, with the source. Ask only when the goal itself
is unknown.

This decides how the work is done. How much gets built still follows "Build only what was asked for".
